# Image Credits & Rights — R.J. Griffin Construction

Per the ZMG Website Compliance Standard §6, every image on this site must be
client-owned, properly licensed, AI-generated by us, or genuinely royalty-free
with the source recorded.

## Owner attestation

R.J. Griffin Construction (owner Ronald Griffin) attests that all gallery,
hero, and before/after photographs used on this website are the company's own
photographs of its own completed projects, used with authorization. No stock
photography or third-party imagery is used for project work.

## Caption / provenance flag — CONFIRM BEFORE RELYING ON

Any caption that names a specific **town**, **neighborhood**, or **project
type** (e.g. "Kitchen remodel in Greece, NY", "Basement egress in Spencerport")
must correspond to a **real job** at that location and of that type. Mislabeling
a photo's location or scope could be a deceptive-advertising exposure under
NY GBL §349/§350 and the FTC Act.

- [ ] Owner to confirm each captioned photo matches a real project at the stated
      town and of the stated type. Correct or remove any caption that does not.
- [ ] Confirm no photo depicts a job actually performed by a subcontractor or
      another contractor if it is presented as R.J. Griffin's own work.

## Note on Facebook-sourced photos

If any image originated from the company Facebook page, note that a patron or a
hired photographer may hold copyright even on the business's own page. Owner
confirmation of ownership/rights (above) covers this, but keep proof (original
files / photographer release) on hand in case of a takedown claim.
