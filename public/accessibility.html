Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 14, 2026

R.J. Griffin Construction is committed to making this website accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What we've done

  • Text and background colors are chosen to meet AA contrast standards.
  • All meaningful images include descriptive alternative text.
  • The site can be navigated and operated using a keyboard, with visible focus indicators.
  • Form fields have clear labels, and buttons and links describe their purpose.
  • The site uses proper headings and page structure for screen readers, and respects reduced-motion preferences.

Ongoing effort

Accessibility is an ongoing process. We test our site and work to fix issues as we find them. Some content may not yet be fully accessible, and we welcome feedback.

Contact us about accessibility

If you have trouble using any part of this website, or you'd like this information in another format, please contact us at rjgriffinconstruction@gmail.com and we'll work to help and to fix the issue promptly.
