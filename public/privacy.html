Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

R.J. Griffin Construction ("we", "us", "our") operates https://rjgriffinconstruction.com. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and your choices. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to respond to you and run this website.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us through our forms. When you submit a contact or estimate request we collect the details you enter, typically your name, email address, phone number, and your message. We ask only for what we need to respond to your inquiry.
  • Basic technical information. Like most websites, our host records standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited) for security and reliability.
  • Analytics. We do not use analytics or tracking cookies.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide the services you ask about.
  • To operate, secure, and improve this website.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

How your form information is handled

When you submit a form, your message is delivered to us using FormSubmit.co, a third-party form-delivery service, and then stored in our email. We share your information only with service providers that help us operate the site (such as our website host and form-delivery service), and only as needed to deliver your message to us.

Cookies and third-party services

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Any cookies present are strictly necessary for the site to function.

Data retention & security

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed to respond and for our reasonable business records, then delete it. Consistent with the New York SHIELD Act, we maintain reasonable safeguards to protect the personal information of New York residents. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have given us by emailing rjgriffinconstruction@gmail.com. If you contacted us and no longer wish to hear back, just let us know.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "effective date" above shows the latest version.
